How it works

Learn by doing, not just reading

We built Glassbox around one idea: the best way to understand AI is to poke at it. Every module includes labs that run right in your browser.

Train a real model

Click to add examples and watch a spam classifier draw its decision boundary. Add a mislabeled point and see what bad data does.

Think like a chatbot

Build a sentence one predicted word at a time using a tiny language model, and see why fluent doesn't mean correct.

Catch the mistakes

Fact-check an AI answer, judge real-world school scenarios, and build a proper APA or MLA citation for AI help.

Why this matters

AI is already in your classroom

Students are using chatbots for homework help, writing feedback and studying, often without anyone explaining how these tools work or where they fail. UNESCO's global guidance on generative AI in education calls on schools to build students' AI literacy so they can use these tools safely, critically and ethically.

Glassbox is our answer: a free, no-login portal written by students, for students, that treats AI as something you can understand and question, not something to fear or blindly trust.
